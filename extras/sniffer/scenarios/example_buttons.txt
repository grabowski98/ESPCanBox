Steering wheel: volume + three times, every 2 s
Steering wheel: volume - three times, every 2 s
Steering wheel: SRC once, wait 3 s
Steering wheel: next track, 3 s, previous track, 3 s
Driver door: open, 3 s, close

Boot: open, 3 s, close
Lights: side lights on, 5 s, off
Start the engine, 10 s without moving

Rear parking sensors: reverse engaged, move an object towards the rear bumper until the continuous tone, take it away, disengage reverse
